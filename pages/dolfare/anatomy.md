## In a Nutshell

<div class="hc ac jsb reflow">

> Dawlfs are mammal humanoid animals, they are fully organic and have magical properties built into their biology.
>
> They are canines, like foxes or dogs, and most are fairly short humanoids.
>
> They have [pointy ears, and fangs poking out of their snout](#head),
> a [gem](#gems) locked around one of their limbs providing them magical
> abilities, as well as a long and bushy [tail](#tail) for most breeds.

> can have one or multiple [traits](../traits), separated from their specific breed.

## Overall Anatomy

This page contains the different details and features common to most dawlf breeds.

For breed specific information check [this page](../breeds/).

### Color Palette

The dolfare often follow the following color palette for their natural
coloration:
- **One to three** fur colors, often different shades of the same tone.
- **One** additional **accent** fur color, far removed from any other and often an
  unnatural tone.
- **One** gem color, which will also color the pupil matching the gem's side, usually
  a vivid shade and tone.
- **One** eye color, or **two** if the dawlf has heterochromia.
- They may also have one or two additional colors for details like flesh, nose,
  nails, etc.

Note that this color palette ignores the existence of fur dyes or other unnatural
oddities that could modify the dawlf's color palette. It is a guideline, not a
hard rule.

The commonest natural fur coloration swatches change per region, they're detailed in the
section for the specific regions of Laraybis.

### Head

The shape of a dolfare's head is round, often with fur tufts on the cheeks.

They have hair on their head on top of the fur covering their whole bodies,
often of a different color from their base fur.

A dawlf's snout is flat and short, with the two upper fangs protrude out at all times

Their ears are pointy and short, often with fur near the ear canal to protect it from getting dirty.

<img src="/media/dolfare/anatomy/head.avif" loading="lazy">

### Paws

A dolfare's hand paws have 3 fingers and an opposable thumb per hand.

Their fingers are short and maneuverable. With pads on each finger and their
palms. Most of them trim their nails into a conic shape so as not to scratch anything by accident.

<img src="/media/dolfare/anatomy/hands.avif" loading="lazy">

A dolfare can be either plantagrade or digitigrade, however plantagrades are
more common.

Dawlf foot paws are elongated with three toes and no thumb, with paw pads on
each toe and a bigger one on the contact area.

<img src="/media/dolfare/anatomy/footpaws.avif" loading="lazy">

> Note: [Aquatic dawlfs](../breeds/aquatic/) have differences for both paws.

### Tail

The tail of a dawlf is mostly made of long empty fur with a small "real" tail much
shorter than where the fur reaches.

The movements of the tail are completely involuntary and the dawlf has no control over them,
mainly moving from side to side when excited or to cool themselves down.

<img src="/media/dolfare/anatomy/tail.avif" loading="lazy">

> Note: [Aquatic](../breeds/aquatic/) and [longtail](../breeds/longtail) dawlfs have differences.

### Gems

Each dawlf is born with a gem-like object **disconnected yet stuck tightly** in one of their limbs.
It grows alongside the dawlf through their life and is made of a smooth, bone-like material.

It is the same color as the pupil of the eye closest to it. If the gem is
centered, it will be asymmetrical towards one side, the side with more gem will
be the one with the colored pupil.

These gems usually take on organic looking shapes of many sizes but don't feature many sharp edges
that could hurt the dawlf. Some dolfare are very prideful about  its shape, color, or shininess.

They channel the magical powers of the dawlf, as well as containing their life-force.

Damaging or scraping the gem would cause great damage to the dawlf, so tripping or modifying it
is considered taboo by the dolfare. Breaking it, however, is quite a difficult
task. If it were to shatter, it would release a violent explosion of the dawlf's concentrated
elemental magic, taking out the dawlf in the process.
