---
title: Dolfare Traits
---

The dolfare may have a number of traits independent of their specific breed.

Not all traits are compatible with all breeds, this is done to keep each breed
different and interesting. For compatibility consult the page of each specific breed.

## Horns

Some dolfare may be born with horns on their head, these are mostly cosmetic,
and do serve as a place for fashionable accessories. Horns always come in pairs, **there are
no natural unicorn dawlfs**.

[Royal](../breeds/royal) dawlfs in particular take pride in their horns if born with them.

There are several places and shapes a dolfare may grow horns, some include:
> - Around the ears
> - Pointed upwards and away from each other
> - Short and pointy nubs
> - Low and horizontal away from the head

## Pouches

Some dolfare grow a pouch over their torsos during their early life, usually along with
larger bodies to carry things in it comfortably.

Despite dawlfs being mammals, they aren't marsupial; so it doesn't serve to carry children
in the early stages of life, but it sure makes carrying them and much more way easier for them!

The origins of these pouches is considered a mystery to the dolfare, their
presence and development seems rather arbitrary. The current theory is that it is
a rare genetic condition passed down from the parents.
