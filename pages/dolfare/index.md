The dolfare (doll-fah-re), or more commonly, "the dawlf" is a **fictional, open, and free to use furry species** designed by FatDawlf,
openly licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/deed.en).

## Introduction

They are short, canine-like creatures native to the fictional world of Laraybis
far away from Earth, or *Terra* as it is known in-universe.

The purpose of this species is to create a **cohesive and believable fantasy
world** in community, where the defining traits of the species, their world, and
other materials are kept open ended so that anyone may continue to expand and deepen
the world and push what a dawlf could even be.

As implied, the dolfare exist within their own universe officially, it's not
explicitly asked to participate in it, but it's highly encouraged to play with
the systems they were designed for. An example of this would be making use of the
**flexible magic system** of **affinities**, one of the 3 magic systems present in
the universe.

## Contributing

You are highly encouraged to contribute any ideas and extensions you may wanna
see to dawlfs or their world.

If you would like to see a dawlf with an interesting new **trait**, a different
**breed**, or an idea for a town or place of interest that would fit within the
world of Laraybis, feel free to discuss it with me to formally add it to the
official species specification.
