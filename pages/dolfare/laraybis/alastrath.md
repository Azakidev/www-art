---
title: Alastrath
---

The volcanic liza, home to the only active volcano of Laraybis and several hot springs.

## Settlements

- Furare
> Once a village for civilians, it's now a somewhat abandoned military town. Most of it was
> destroyed back when it was a civilian village in a fire and was never rebuilt afterward.
>
> Some say it was caused by the former emperor Larabiel, they're probably right.

## Points of Interest

- Lateur volcano
> The largest of the island, somewhat active. Its surface is dotted with large
> boulders.

- Slataur volcano
> The closest one to Furare, inactive. Dead bushes line it's base.

- Retiur volcano
> The furthest from Furare, it's been long dormant, yet it's been partially
> destroyed by a large explosion long ago, producing a lake of standing lava.

- Agonia-Alastrath tunnel
> An artificial tunnel, used by the military for transport and not much else since it runs
> rather hot due to the volcanic activity

- Fusha hot springs
> A collection of extensive and vast hot spring lakes. They used to be quite
> popular, although they've become quite a dangerous place to access.
