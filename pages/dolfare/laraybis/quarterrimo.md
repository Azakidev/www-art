---
title: Quarterrimo
---

The capital of Laraybis, a large circular island in the middle of the archipelago that communicates
every island via speedrail, contains an elevator to [Agonia](../agonia), and hosts hitaki taxi
services to reach [Sirroma](../sirroma).

## Settlements

- Quarterrimo city
> A large city spanning most of Quarterrimo, it's bustling with people from all
> liza trading, living, or just visiting as a tourist.
>
> Many neighborhoods around the city show stark cultural and technological
> differences, remnants of the city's growth from a handful of villages.

## Points of Interest

- The Imperial Palace
> While called a palace, the emperors don't live there as it's a remnant of the monarchy that
> came before them.
>
> It has since been adapted into the workplace of most of the court, leaving
> some areas untouched for historical purposes.

- Laraybis Plaza
> A large circular plaza located at the core of the city center.
>
> It is home to the most populous market of the zone, and the beauty of it's
> masonry makes it quite the popular choice for lovebirds seeking to propose
> their love.

- Queen's museum
> Where most of the relics from the queen's time lay here, as well as artwork
> from the era.

- Museum of the new arts
> This museum was built after the Queen's museum got too full, it unified a few smaller
> galleries into a larger museum.
>
> It was rather overbuilt, so it often hosts contests and community events.
