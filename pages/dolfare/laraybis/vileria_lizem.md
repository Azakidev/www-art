---
title: Vileria Lizem
---

An archipelago of rather small lizem, house to a small village with the only known
population of aquatic dawlfs.

## Settlements

- Sulare village
> This village is rather small, lining the coast of the bigger islands of the archipelago,
> travel between them is by boat or simply by swimming.

## Points of Interest

- Machata creek
> An underwater volcanically active zone north of the Vileria islands, used as a source of
> geothermal energy when needed.

- Vileria coral reef
> A coral reef east of the islands that sprawls nearly a square kilometer in area.
