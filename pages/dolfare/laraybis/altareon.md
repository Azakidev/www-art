---
title: Altareon
---

The mountainous liza, covered in cliffs and hills. Source of freshwater and origin of the
Agonia tunnel system.

## Settlements

- The tundra tribes
> While not an established settlement per se, several groups of longtail dawlfs
> peregrinated to the tundra and among the mountains, making their own little
> villages.

## Points of Interest

- The tundras
> A large portion of the liza is coated by a thick tundra. Given it's one of the
> few spots in the entirety of Laraybis, it makes for quite the novel experience
> for many southern dawlfs.

- Kala cliff
> A hollow cliff on the east coast of the island, flooded with ocean water, leading to a cave
> with a freshwater underground lake inside.

- Agonia passage
> The only natural cave that leads to Agonia from the surface and wasn't turned to an elevator
> for historical preservation purposes.

- Mella waterworks
> A water production facility, tasked with getting fresh water and distributing it across Laraybis.
