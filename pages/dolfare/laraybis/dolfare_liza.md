---
title: Dolfare Liza
---

The virgin liza. It is said to be the birthplace of dawlfs as the modern
[feral](../../breeds/feral) counterpart of dawlfs still live there after millions of years,
seemingly unchanged.

Dawlfs treat it as a sacred place and are very protective of any and all visitors getting
close to it, as it serves them as a reminder of their origins.

## Settlements

There are no settlements in this region, the only ones living here are the feral dawlfs,
since residence of humanoid dawlfs is strictly forbidden.

## Points of Interest

- Dolfare sanctorio
> A sanctuary to feral dawlfs. It's placed near a natural spring of water in the middle
> of a forest, a couple of places to sit down, and not much else.
>
> It's the only place in the island, besides the bridge leading up to it, that has been
> touched by humanoid dawlfs.
