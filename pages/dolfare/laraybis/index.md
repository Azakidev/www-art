---
title: The World of Laraybis
---

Laraybis is the planet dawlfs reside in.

The planetoid is a half spheroid with a magical dome called "the barrier" atop its
flat side, on it is an archipelago of several landmasses or "liza" as named by the dolfare.

The demonym for Laraybis is laraybean.
