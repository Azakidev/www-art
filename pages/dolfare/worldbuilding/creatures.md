---
title: On the Creatures of Laraybis
---

Here are some of the other creatures that populate Laraybis.

---

## The Hitaki

Horse like im nature and looking like a feathery lizard, these winged beasts were tamed by
the dolfare time immemorial.

They nest on Sirroma and serve as the main mode of transportation on air.

---

## The Koo

Large avian creatures featuing long wingspans and elongated bodies.

They like to fly in groups above humid environments, since they feed on fish and
other aquatic animals.

---

## The Sliverette

These curious creatures lurk in tall grass, they're shifty and rodent-like, living mostly
over at the source half of Armatreus.

Their appearance seems to be middle ground of squirrels, rats, and ferrets, though their
diet is more omnivorous, often hunting smaller rodents.

A winged variant can be found in the east area of Peritio, akin to winged squirrels, which
helps them navigate the dangerous cliff side where they live and nest.

---

## The Chattu

They're basically chickens but they're gray and a little bit larger than chicken.
They can fly and are not to be messed with when hungry.

Fortunately, they have been tamed by the dolfare and are used as one of the most
popular meat types.

---

## The Limeros

Large lemur-like rodents that live atop the treetops of the Altareon tundra, using skin
flaps to fly from tree to tree to stalk prey and flee from predators such as ancient dolfare.
