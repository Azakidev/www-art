---
title: Longtail
---

The natives of the Altareon tundra.

Larger than average, usually lanky with long limbs and much taller than any
other breed. Longtail dolfare have, as their name implies, longer tails than the other breeds,
with a longer and more muscular real tail that allows for voluntary motion and even the ability to
use their tails as an additional movement option.

They originated from the crossbreeding of [royal](../royal) and [plain](../plain) dawlfs long ago,
the traits that made royals slightly taller happened to remain and become even more prevalent,
eventually stabilizing and branching off [bastard](../bastard) dawlfs into their own breed.
