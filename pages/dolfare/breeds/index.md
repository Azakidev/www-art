title: Dolfare Breeds
---

The dolfare come in a variety of different breeds, giving them distinct magical
and physical abilities, as well as unique differences to others.

|         Name          |             Specialty             |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| [Plain](plain)        | The baseline                      |
| [Royal](royal)        | Taller and stronger magically     |
| [Longtail](longtail)  | Taller and stronger physically    |
| [Arid](arid)          | Adapted to extreme desert heat    |
| [Aquatic](aquatic)    | Adapted to water and swimming     |
| [Bastard](bastard)    | An unstable crossbreed            |
| [Feral](feral)        | The oldest quadrupeds             |

## Crossbreeds

Crossbreeds are a common occurrence among dawlfs aside for royals.

For a crossbreed, a union of the different special traits from each breed is
selected, with one often being more dominantly expressed than the other. The
specifics are left open ended so that character designers may take artistic
liberty.
