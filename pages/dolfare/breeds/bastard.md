A forbidden crossbreed.

Bastard dawlfs are the result of crossbreeding between a [royal](../royal) and a [plain](../plain)
dolfare. They're often unstable in form and trend to live less than the others,
for that they are considered taboo by most dolfare.

They're not considered a breed by itself, as it's only used in the first couple of generations,
if the bastard dawlf even lasts that long, before eventually leading to a [royal](../royal),
[longtail](../longtail), or [plain](../plain) dawlf.
