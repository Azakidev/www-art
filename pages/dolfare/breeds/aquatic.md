---
title: Aquatic
---

Residents of the [Vileria lizem](../../laraybis/vileria_lizem).

These dawlfs resemble otters, having a much slimmer and quite different look to any other
breed of dawlf.

Aquatic dolfare are always plantagrade. They replaced their tail with a nub, their fingers joined by a skin flaps to aid in swimming. In fact, aquatic dolfare are **the only breed that can swim**.

They are local to the small coastal village of Sulare, lining the coasts of the
[Vileria lizem](../../laraybis/vileria_lizem).

## Stats

|     Key       |     Value     |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Heights       | 1.45 - 1.6 m  |
| Weights       | 40 - 60 kg    |
| Tail length   | 4 - 8 cm      |
