---
title: Plain
---

The most basic and abundant version of dawlfs.

They are often grouped with breeds that derived from them to refer to the dawlfs
outside of the [royals](../royal).

Plain dawlfs are most common in [Armatreus](../../laraybis/armatreus) and
[Quarterrimo](../.../laraybis/quarterrimo), their color palette being among the
most varying, anywhere from black or whites, to blues, browns, lavenders, the
sky is the limit for this diverse crowd.

## Stats

|     Key       |     Value     |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Heights       | 1.3 - 1.4 m   |
| Weights       | 50 - 70 kg    |
| Tail length   | 35 - 45 cm    |
