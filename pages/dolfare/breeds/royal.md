title: Royal
---

The high dawlf society.

These dawlfs are more powerful magically, slightly taller than the regular dawlfs,
and have access to a 3rd elemental affinity.

Royal dawlfs are most common in [Quarterrimo](../../laraybis/quarterrimo), but
some can be spotted in any liza [plain](../plain) dawlfs can be spotted at.

## Stats

|      Key      |     Value     |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Heights       | 1.4 - 1.6 m   |
| Weights       | 50 - 70 kg    |
| Tail length   | 40 - 55 cm    |
