The purest descendants of the ancient dolfare.

These dawlfs are the descendants of the ancient original dawlfs, who were separated of the rest by
staying at [Dolfare liza](../../laraybis/dolfare_liza), preserving the ancient form of dawlfs
to this day.

Although they aren't considered endangered, their population is considerably smaller than their
humanoid counterparts.

## Stats

|     Key       |     Value     |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| Heights       | 1 - 1.3 m     |
| Weights       | 10 - 15 kg    |
| Tail length   | 20 - 30 cm    |
