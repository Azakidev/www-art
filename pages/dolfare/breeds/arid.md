title: Arid
---

The spiritual society of the [Peritio](../../laraybis/peritio) dessert.

These dawlfs have shorter and thinner fur than other breeds, with bald spots dotted around.
They also boast a much nimbler build, a side effect of the rough war-torn terrain they reside among.

Due to their lifestyle, arid dawlfs possess a heightened magical ability due to their
spiritual living.

Arid dawlfs usually spot lighter colors on their fur, be it cream, yellow, or
even pink. The lighter hues help deal with the sun, even if most wear robes and
hoods to protect themselves.
